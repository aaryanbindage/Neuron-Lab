    Words you'll see

    Neuron
    A small calculator. It takes numbers in, mixes them, and passes one number out.
    Weight
    A dial for one input. A big positive weight says "this matters a lot", a negative one says "this argues against".
    Bias
    A head start added to the sum. A high bias makes the neuron say yes easily; a low one makes it hard to convince.
    Activation function
    A rule that reshapes the sum into the neuron's output, for example squashing it between 0 and 1.
    Layer
    A column of neurons that work side by side. Layers between the input and the output are called hidden.
    Training
    Showing the network examples, measuring its mistakes (the loss), and nudging every weight a little to make fewer of them.
